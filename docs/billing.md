# WTES billing foundation — not release-ready

The offer is $14.99/month after a 30-day no-card trial beginning with the first saved own-data summary. Signing in or viewing the unrestricted demo does not start it. A successful checkout is not itself proof of entitlement; the server accepts a completed transaction only after checking the dedicated price, a server-stored nonce, its specific transaction ID, and the customer/subscription against Paddle. A signed webhook synchronizes subscription state. Only `active` is a candidate for paid access; trial status is separately sourced from the workspace.

Configuration requires dedicated WTES values: `WTES_PADDLE_ENV` (`sandbox` or `production`), `WTES_PADDLE_API_KEY`, `WTES_PADDLE_CLIENT_TOKEN`, `WTES_PADDLE_PRICE_MONTHLY`, `WTES_PADDLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `WTES_BILLING_BLOB_TOKEN` (private store), and `WTES_BILLING_SECRET` (independent random secret at least 32 bytes). WorkOS and workspace settings are separate. No provider accounts, products, prices, keys, notification destinations or environment settings were changed. The WTES Paddle price must be checked for the approved amount, billing interval and absence of an additional Paddle trial, and the approved default payment link must load Paddle.js. Configure notifications to the exact `https://<WTES host>/api/billing/webhook` endpoint only after resolving the release blockers below.

`/billing` is the persistent signed-in entry point for checkout and self-service management; the server creates a fresh Paddle portal session for the authenticated account and validates the destination host. Checkout and portal require an exact same-origin POST. Trial data and subscription records are independent; deleting workspace contents does not delete the billing record or cancel a subscription. Webhook signatures cover the raw body with HMAC SHA-256 and a five-minute timestamp window. State is encrypted at rest, bound to a server-created transaction, and updated with optimistic concurrency and provider event time. Events may arrive before the transaction is bound or completed and return non-2xx to preserve Paddle retry.

## Release blockers and verification

- Approved full refunds and chargebacks place a conservative access hold. Reversals trigger a fresh Paddle lookup for every held adjustment; only adjustments whose authoritative status is reversed are cleared, and access resumes only if the matching subscription is active and no holds remain. Partial refunds do not automatically end access. Test all cases with real sandbox notifications before release.
- A failed transaction creation reservation can be retried after 15 minutes; an existing transaction is reused and a canceled subscription can start a new checkout. Verify those paths and avoid duplicate subscriptions in sandbox before release.
- Verify sandbox and then authorized production: real $14.99 price and tax display, first successful own-data save and 30-day boundary, no-card trial, paid purchase, matching account ownership, webhook retry/duplicate/out-of-order handling, refunds and chargebacks, cancel and management portal, browser refresh and two-user isolation. No checkout is confirmed by a client callback alone.
- Inspect public privacy terms, pricing disclosure, CSP (Paddle scripts and frames), mobile, accessibility, actual browser behavior, measurement privacy, and account-deletion and retention behavior. The runtime is deployed and its hosted build passed 165 tests and compilation/type checking; production route/viewport checks do not verify billing. Paddle configuration and the real billing lifecycle remain unconfigured and untested. Do not reuse Review Intel secrets or customer data.

Paddle references: https://developer.paddle.com/api-reference/transactions/create-transaction/ ; https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/about/how-webhooks-work/ ; https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/adjustments/adjustment-created/ .

Before creating a checkout the server fetches the configured price and requires an active USD 1499-cent price, monthly interval, and no Paddle trial. WorkOS login requires a verified email and validates the returned RS256 token against WorkOS keys; the access session expires within five minutes or the provider token expiry, whichever is sooner, and an encrypted HttpOnly refresh cookie rotates through WorkOS while the user is active. Provider-side revocation can take that long to take effect. The real WorkOS and Paddle provider lifecycles have not been exercised.

`WTES_BILLING_ENABLED=true` together with a valid UTC ISO `WTES_BILLING_STARTS_AT` at or before the current time enables billing; a missing, invalid or future launch timestamp leaves billing off. With the switch absent or false, saving remains available as a free beta and purchase controls are hidden. When enabled, incomplete Paddle configuration fails closed for saves; after a started trial expires, only an active paid subscription permits further workspace writes. The first save remains available to start the trial. Reading, exporting, deleting saved workspace content, and the subscription portal remain available without paid entitlement; the free browser demo is unaffected. AI/MCP reads are not blocked by the save gate. Verify these rules with configured provider accounts before activation.

## Beta-to-paid activation requirement

Beta saves retain their original stored trial dates. Without modifying those records, the effective trial starts at the later of the first saved summary and the configured paid launch timestamp, and ends 30 days later. An account with no saved summary has no trial until its first save. Verify beta, post-launch and exact-boundary cases before activation; the timestamp is not set by this change. Billing-off screens must describe current free beta accurately. The checkout server now requires an existing trial end and refuses checkout until that instant; no transaction is created for a first-time or still-trialing account. A canceled account can see both resubscription and the existing management entry. A replacement reservation retains the previous customer and subscription IDs for the portal. If creation has an ambiguous outcome before a transaction ID is saved, it fails closed and requires manual reconciliation rather than creating another transaction. Verify portal continuity, abandoned and completed replacement flows, and duplicate prevention in sandbox before activation.

Replacement checkout retains prior subscription/customer/nonce bindings in encrypted billing state, including after the new subscription completes. Signed late subscription events and provider-bound late adjustments for a retained previous subscription cannot alter the replacement's entitlement or holds. Unmatched bindings fail closed; the record caps retained replacements at 32 and requires review beyond that. The older adjustment is acknowledged without applying it to the newer subscription. Verify real Paddle renewal and adjustment payloads, old-event ordering, replacement completion, and portal behaviour in the dedicated sandbox before enabling billing. Test fixtures do not prove provider lifecycle behaviour.

## Owner-only sandbox harness (source only)

A separate disabled-by-default production-host harness uses a verified signed-in owner and an exact server-side `WTES_BILLING_TEST_OWNER_USER_ID`. It requires `WTES_BILLING_TEST_ENABLED=true`, `WTES_BILLING_TEST_PADDLE_API_KEY`, `WTES_BILLING_TEST_PADDLE_CLIENT_TOKEN`, `WTES_BILLING_TEST_PADDLE_PRICE_MONTHLY`, `WTES_BILLING_TEST_PADDLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `WTES_BILLING_TEST_SECRET` (32 bytes minimum), `WTES_BILLING_TEST_BLOB_TOKEN`, and `WTES_BILLING_TEST_PHASE` (`active` or `expired`). The provider URL is hardcoded to Paddle sandbox. A separate private store is required. No test settings have been configured by this source change.

`/api/billing-test/trial` writes an encrypted synthetic first-save marker in the isolated test namespace, with age determined by the server-only phase; the actual account workspace and its trial are not modified. Active and expired fixtures use distinct marker paths. Checkout reuses the production engine and guard with that synthetic trial. Test records use their own prefix and the sandbox webhook uses its own signing secret and owner binding. Test-only results are not evidence that 30 real days have elapsed. Never put production credentials in test settings. Portal links accept only Paddle’s exact environment-specific host: `sandbox-customer-portal.paddle.com` for sandbox and `customer-portal.paddle.com` for live. [Paddle documents both](https://developer.paddle.com/changelog/2025/subscription-management-links-customer-portal/). Verify dedicated store CAS behaviour, webhook delivery, isolation and real provider lifecycle before any claim or activation. No real payment is authorized.
