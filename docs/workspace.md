# WTES account and workspace foundation

This is a separate WorkOS AuthKit application and a separate private Blob store. Configure `WTES_WORKOS_CLIENT_ID`, `WTES_WORKOS_API_KEY`, `WTES_SESSION_SECRET` (at least 32 random bytes), `WTES_APP_ORIGIN` (exact HTTPS origin without trailing slash), `WTES_WORKSPACE_BLOB_TOKEN`, and `WTES_WORKSPACE_SECRET` (at least 32 independent random bytes). Do not reuse Review Intel credentials or storage. Register the exact callback `https://<WTES host>/api/account/callback` in the WTES WorkOS application, enable AuthKit, and use a private Blob store. No provider or environment setup is performed by this change. Missing configuration returns unavailable rather than simulated sign-in.

WorkOS authorization code uses state and S256 PKCE; transient flow and five-minute app session cookies are encrypted, HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. The access session lasts at most five minutes; an encrypted HttpOnly refresh cookie rotates through WorkOS and the application refreshes it while active. Provider revocation may take until the short access session expires. Logout clears local cookies and requests WorkOS session revocation; a provider error is reported. Verify account deletion, revocation and incident response before release.

Only bounded, allowlisted numeric snapshots and enum/date reminder and experiment records are stored, encrypted in private Blob under an HMAC-derived user path. No raw CSV or transaction identities are accepted. Optional user-supplied product labels are private workspace aggregates; users must avoid customer names or other personal information in labels. Labels are limited to 120 characters with control characters removed. They are available to Claude or Codex only when the explicit `shareProductNamesWithAssistant` setting is true; integrations must enforce this on every model or MCP response. Consent defaults to false and can be changed in the workspace. PUT is a full replacement with revision-based conflict detection; reload on 409. The first successful nonempty snapshot save starts the 30-day trial; signing in and saving empty records do not start it. Trial dates persist if snapshots are later deleted. Trial tracking is not a payment or entitlement enforcement system. No card is requested or automatically charged. Reminders are in-dashboard records only; no email or notification is sent.

Before release, verify the real WorkOS round trip, denial and expired state, CSRF rejection, two-account isolation, private Blob encryption and conflicts, first-save trial and boundary, logout, data deletion/retention process, and mobile/account UI. Verify provider configuration and secrets in the intended production environment; do not put them into source or client code. Check public privacy disclosures, analytics exclusions, accessibility and the applicable public launch checklist. Hosted checks are pending; no local tests, build, preview, deployment or external account changes were performed in this handoff.

Snapshot `source` is a client-reported import label, not server-verified PayPal provenance. Month and opaque item identifiers provide numeric summaries without retaining labels; opaque IDs are scoped to the submitted snapshot and do not establish cross-snapshot product identity. Reminder and test finding IDs come from the fixed playbook; derive display names from the playbook. Never infer provider verification from the source label.

Provider reference: https://workos.com/docs/reference/authkit/authentication/get-authorization-url and https://workos.com/docs/reference/authkit/authentication .

`DELETE /api/workspace` requires the authenticated session and exact same origin. It clears snapshots, labels, reminders, tests and name-sharing consent, while retaining encrypted trial dates and a revision tombstone to prevent trial resets and stale writes. It does not delete the WorkOS account or sign the user out. A simultaneous write can return 409; reload before retrying. Provider account deletion and retention policy need separate implementation and verification.

Product labels must be explicitly reviewed (`labelReviewed: true`) and cannot contain obvious email addresses, phone numbers or URLs. The checks cannot detect all personal information. Legacy unreviewed labels are stripped on read. Coverage gaps use fixed codes and are retained with snapshots.
